Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

What we store

Sumic stores the account name you enter and your listening preferences locally in your browser so your library and playback state can persist between visits.

Third-party services

Search, playback, and playlist import may send your query or a public link to the relevant third-party service. Their policies apply to those services.

Your choices

You can clear your browser storage to remove locally saved Sumic data. Do not enter information you would not want processed by the services you use for playback or import.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date and text will be published here.
